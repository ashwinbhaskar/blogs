Stop 1: How this deck works

Reveal one idea at a time

  • Anything with class="frag" stays hidden until the presenter advances.
  • Steps reveal in document order. Add data-order="2" to change the order.
  • A step can also switch something else on: data-target="#some-svg .hl-row" adds the class on to whatever matches.
  • Refer to another slide by its id and the number fills itself in: see slide .
